Game info: single hand per round, 6-deck shoe. The dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack when showing a ten or an Ace. Machine mode reshuffles before every hand; Table mode keeps one shoe and reshuffles at about 75% penetration. Blackjack payout is selectable (2:1, 3:2, or 6:5). Martingale mode doubles the bet after a loss and resets to your chosen denomination after a win; a push repeats the same bet. Flat mode keeps the base bet constant regardless of outcome. A double-down win pays even money on the doubled bet (not double-counted). Splitting is available in Table mode only: pairs can be re-split up to 4 hands, doubling after a split is allowed, and split Aces get one card each and can’t be re-split. Auto and Fast modes always use standard basic strategy. Manual mode shows whether your decisions matched basic strategy.
